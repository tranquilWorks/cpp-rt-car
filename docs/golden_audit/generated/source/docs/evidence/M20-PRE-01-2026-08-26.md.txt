## Implemented assurance lane

`scripts/verify-portable-assurance.sh` exposes fail-closed `dependencies`,
`static`, `fuzz`, `artifacts`, and cumulative `all` modes. It requires an
explicit build/output root and complete expected source commit. Generated
reports and candidate bytes remain below that root.

- Dependency reconciliation covers the clean exact GoogleTest submodule,
  GoogleTest fallback digest, RapidCheck full commit, vcpkg baseline and empty
  lock, exact FetchContent inventory, and every workflow action. Pull-request
  dependency review is blocking at high severity.
- The static gate reconciles 29 exact `target|source` translation units against
  a clean default non-experimental, tests-off, examples-off, demo-off Clang 14
  compilation database and runs analyzer core, C++, dead-code, security, Unix,
  and variadic-list checks as errors. Only the named padding-optimization check
  is excluded because frozen/public records cannot be reordered here.
- The fuzz gate replays immutable seeds, generates the exact maximum boundary,
  and runs fixed seed 424242 under Clang 14 libFuzzer plus ASan/UBSan. Snapshot
  and runtime-profile inputs are capped at 65,536 bytes and 20,000 mutations;
  the explicitly experimental job queue is capped at 4,096 bytes and 10,000
  mutations. Compile/link flags, tool identity, sanitizer settings, corpus and
  dictionary hashes, counts, limits, status, and failure inventory are recorded.
- Artifact mode builds and stages the default Release package, creates two
  byte-identical canonical SPDX 2.3 candidate SBOMs, creates an unsigned
  in-toto Statement v1 candidate, creates and verifies a strict canonical final
  manifest against the caller's exact source commit, verifies all candidate
  bytes offline, extracts safely, runs the relocated consumer, and checks C ABI
  v8 plus SONAME 8.
- A fictional CC0 public fixture exercises actual offline DSSE/RSA PKCS#1 v1.5
  SHA-256 verification plus artifact, signature, trust, repository, source,
  ref, workflow, issuer, and predicate mutation rejection. No private key or
  signing path is retained.

## Claim boundary, risk, and rollback

This evidence may support only the exact local static/tool-test/dependency and
public-fixture results above plus later named hosted results. A bounded seed
smoke is not continuous fuzzing or vulnerability absence. Pins and an SBOM are
not vulnerability, legal, license, or upstream trust clearance. The RTFW
statement is unsigned and unauthenticated; the separate signed fixture does not
authenticate RTFW, establish current trust/revocation state, or create a SLSA
level. No reproducible-build, controlled-performance, soak, physical
CUDA/XDMA/combined, HIL, field, latency, RT1/RT2, Unreal, signed-release,
publication, deployment, or production claim follows.

Rollback is one coherent revert of the M20-PRE-01 verification, policies,
corpora, public-only fixture, CI wiring, docs/contracts/digests, and this
evidence. Generated temporary/CI candidates may be discarded after confirming
they were never tagged, published, installed system-wide, or used for support
or qualification. Any security issue exposed by the lane remains a release
blocker even if the tooling is reverted.
