## Review, rollback and limits

Review covers byte/region layout, atomic publication, one-writer ownership,
clock-domain and overflow boundaries, input/output transactionality, checked
Runtime stop before local unmap, remote-view survival, subprocess cleanup and
installed entry points. No unresolved implementation finding remains. All final gates remain required before integration.

Peers are trusted: no malicious-writer protection/authentication or cross-host
clock synchronization is provided. Fresh name/generation uniqueness is caller
policy; no in-place recovery, claim reuse or crash daemon exists. A killed Linux
creator can leave an orphan name requiring exact-session administrative cleanup.
Host freshness is measured at frame entry, not callback completion. Ordinary OS
scheduling/polling makes no hard-real-time guarantee. Windows validates its requested
4096-byte view and declared header, not a larger section's native extent. TSan
same-process evidence does not prove cross-process race freedom. These limits are
also explicit in the shipped guide.

Rollback is a scoped revert of the additive kit, package/test wiring and docs.
Preserve all production/SDK/ABI and previous evidence. No migration is required.
Independent novice review, physical HIL/CUDA/XDMA, RT1/RT2, controlled performance,
Unreal and signing/release/deployment remain unperformed and separate. Remaining
software after this batch: M25-05 independent host adapter, M25-06 installed
API/docs/recipes and six M26 full-system batches.
