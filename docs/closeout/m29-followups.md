## Ownership limits

`request_stop` closes admission; a callback already admitted may still finish.
Direct callers, like Runtime checked stop, must quiesce callbacks before changing
registered buffers or resetting, shutting down, reinitializing, moving or destroying
the backend. This repair adds no arbitrary concurrent lifecycle guarantee and
no rollback of caller buffer writes. A bounded cancel scan may return
`invalid_argument` while submit or another consumer owns the relevant slot.
Callers retain their existing terminal polling and checked cleanup obligations.
Experimental full owners retain FMA-off, caller-thread arena ownership and the
experimental support boundary. Passing that consumer does not prove concurrent
global policy changes or qualify SimCore as the supported portable Runtime.
